# OVERWRITTEN: curated profile

A faceless interactive horror series about a courier whose online life is copied until the copy is trusted more
than he is. This profile is the reference setup the kit was built and tested on (season 1, 12 episodes).

## Locked choices
- **Style:** faceless noir. Night palette: ink `#0b0f14`, blue-grey `#27313b`, bone `#f1eee9` (heads, captions),
  ember `#fd8358` (living people, Ilo's scarf), cyan `#35e0d0` (fakes and links), red `#e5484d` (countdowns only).
- **Cast:** `cast.js` here. Ilo is known by the orange scarf, Mum by the rust cardigan and cream apron, Tam by the
  green bomber and flip phone. Every head is a plain bone circle with an ink outline.
- **Voices:** `series.json` here. Narrator am_michael at 1.1. A copy of a character uses the same voice with the
  fake treatment at 0.3.
- **Interactive:** every episode ends on an A/B card. The next episode opens on the wrong answer, which ends in Ilo
  dying or being taken (cyan flood, black, an ember flatline), then a real rewind (the wrong turn plays backwards on
  its own frames, tape whirr), then the right answer.
- **Hook and title:** a one-line first-person hook from Ilo (3 to 5 s), then the OVERWRITTEN title card (typed over
  scrambled cyan glyphs, scan bar, red rule, SEASON 1 · EPISODE N, title typed on).
- **Sound:** one continuous dark bed (90 s CC0 drone), drone layer by marks, heartbeat ranges, SFX on every cut,
  ping per message, buzz per call, lock clunks, TV hum, sub drop and stinger on the death, tape rewind.
- **Camera:** wide (+26 degrees over the old framing on scenes, +8 on phone inserts), whips are cuts, faint drift.

## Season 1 shape
Twelve 60 to 75 s episodes. Each teaches one rule of the threat and adds at most one person. Mum is lost to the copy
in episode 7. The season ends before the bigger threats exist.
